What we teach

Five departments. Tap one to see what it covers.

Qur'an memorizationMemorize with accuracy, recite with beautyالقرآن
  • Hifz (memorization)Start from the beginning or continue what you already know, at a steady pace.
  • TajweedThe rules of correct recitation, from letter articulation to lengthening and stops.
  • Recitation practiceLearn to read fluently with correct pronunciation.
  • Revision (muraja'ah)Review plans so what you memorize stays with you.
Arabic languageUnderstand the language of the Qur'anالعربية
  • Reading and writingThe alphabet, vowels and connecting letters, from zero.
  • VocabularyEveryday words and the words that appear most in the Qur'an.
  • Grammar and word formsNahw and sarf, explained step by step.
  • Speaking and comprehensionSimple conversation and understanding Arabic texts.
Islamic studiesKnow your faith from reliable sourcesالإسلامية
  • AqeedahThe foundations of belief.
  • FiqhThe rulings of prayer, fasting, zakat, hajj and daily life.
  • Hadith and TafsirUnderstanding the Sunnah and the meanings of the Qur'an.
  • Seerah and AdabThe life of the Prophet ﷺ and the character that follows from it.
Western studiesModern academic knowledge, taught with good valuesالغربية
  • English languageReading, writing and speaking.
  • MathematicsFrom the basics upward, for school-age learners and adults.
  • SciencesHow the natural world works.
  • Social studies and historyUnderstanding the world and its past.
TechnologyPractical technology skills, learned onlineالتقنية
  • Computer and internet basicsUsing devices, files and the web with confidence.
  • Digital safetyProtecting yourself and your family online.
  • Everyday digital toolsTyping, documents, email and online learning tools.
  • Web design and programmingBuild websites and learn to code.

How learning works here

  1. Your class is setYou chose your classes when you joined. They decide your subjects and timetable.
  2. Open your subjectsEach subject shows its teacher and its book.
  3. Join your lessons onlineYour timetable shows each lesson in its own country's time, and in yours.
  4. Use coins for books and coursesYour wallet keeps your coins. Add more from the wallet page.

Questions

Where do I find my books?

Open Subjects. Each subject has its book. If you have not unlocked it yet, the button shows its price in coins.

Where is my timetable?

Open Timetable. The admin sets it for each class, term by term.

How do I pay for books and courses?

With coins from your wallet. Open My wallet to add coins.

Can I change my class?

Your class is fixed once you choose it. Contact the admin if it needs to change.
