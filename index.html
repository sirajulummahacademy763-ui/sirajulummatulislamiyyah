Questions

Is it free to create an account?

Yes. Creating an account costs nothing. Some books and courses use coins from your wallet.

Do I need any experience?

No. Every department starts from the beginning.

How do I register?

Tap Register now, fill in the short form, then choose your classes.

When does registration close?

Registration closes on the day a term begins and opens again after the term ends. The dates are at the top of this page.

Can I learn on my phone?

Yes. The classes, library and exams all work on a phone or a computer.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes. Certificates can be checked online by anyone you show them to.

Where are my books?

In Subjects. Each subject shows its book and its price in coins.

Where is my timetable?

In Timetable. The admin sets it for your class, term by term.

How do I get coins?

Open My wallet and add coins.

Can I change my class?

Contact the admin. Your class is fixed once chosen.
