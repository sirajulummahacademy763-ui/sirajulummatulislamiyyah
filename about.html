About the institute

Sirajul Ummah Islamic Institute offers Qur'an memorization, Islamic and Arabic studies, Western studies and technology, all online and at each learner's own pace.

Our Mission

To teach the Qur'an, Arabic and Islamic knowledge alongside Western education and technology, online and at each learner's own pace. We guide children, teenagers and adults to learn with accuracy, good character and confidence.

Our Vision

A world where every Muslim learner, wherever they live, can memorize and understand the Qur'an and gain modern knowledge and skills, without leaving their faith or their home.

Our Ambition

To grow into a leading online Islamic institute, with complete classes from Rawdatul Atfal to Thanawi and from KG to SSS 3, and a free library for the world. We aim to raise graduates who serve their families and communities with knowledge and good values.

What we stand for

1

Authentic learning

Everything we teach is built on reliable Islamic sources: the Qur'an, the authentic Sunnah, and the understanding of the trusted scholars of the Ummah. Our teachers are qualified, our books are checked, and where a question needs a scholar's ruling, we send learners to the right person.

2

Accessible education

Knowledge should not depend on where you live or how much you can pay. Learners study from their phone or computer, at any hour, from the very first letter to advanced classes, with a free library open to the whole world and simple, fair prices for our class books.

3

Structured study

Every learner follows a clear path: classes from Rawdatul Atfal to Thanawi and from KG to SSS 3, step-by-step lessons, live classes with teachers, assignments, marks and feedback. Learners always know where they are, what comes next, and how they are improving.

4

Good character

Knowledge is not complete without practice. We teach adab, honesty, respect for parents and teachers, patience and kindness alongside every subject, so that our students become people who benefit their families, their neighbours and their communities.

Meet the founder

Abdullahi Miftaudeen

Founder, Sirajul Ummah Islamic Institute
Student, Kashful Ulum College

Alhamdulillah. I have always thought deeply about teaching, and this led me to open my first physical madrasah, Sirajul Ummah Institute. Unfortunately, some people could not join us because of the distance.

So I began to ask myself: how can I bring the madrasah online? After thinking about it, I shared the idea with my dear friend, Sheikh Abdul Basit, the founder of Alpha Tech Hub. He was very happy about it and began working on the website straight away.

Alhamdulillah, my dream has now become a reality. Sirajul Ummah Institute has both a physical madrasah and an online institute, so that anyone, wherever they are, can learn. We ask Allah to accept this effort and make it a means of benefit for the Ummah.
