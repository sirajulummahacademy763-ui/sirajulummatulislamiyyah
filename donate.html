A gift that keeps giving. Help us teach the Qur'an and Islamic knowledge to more people, and let every lesson learned become a reward that keeps reaching you.

The words of Allah and His Messenger ﷺ

Giving for the sake of Allah is never lost. Read what He and His Prophet ﷺ said about it.

مَّثَلُ الَّذِينَ يُنفِقُونَ أَمْوَالَهُمْ فِي سَبِيلِ اللَّهِ كَمَثَلِ حَبَّةٍ أَنبَتَتْ سَبْعَ سَنَابِلَ فِي كُلِّ سُنبُلَةٍ مِّائَةُ حَبَّةٍ ۗ وَاللَّهُ يُضَاعِفُ لِمَن يَشَاءُ ۗ وَاللَّهُ وَاسِعٌ عَلِيمٌ

"The example of those who spend their wealth in the way of Allah is like a seed that grows seven ears, in every ear a hundred grains. And Allah multiplies for whom He wills, and Allah is Vast and All-Knowing."

Qur'an, Surah Al-Baqarah 2:261

إِذَا مَاتَ الإِنْسَانُ انْقَطَعَ عَنْهُ عَمَلُهُ إِلاَّ مِنْ ثَلاَثَةٍ: إِلاَّ مِنْ صَدَقَةٍ جَارِيَةٍ، أَوْ عِلْمٍ يُنْتَفَعُ بِهِ، أَوْ وَلَدٍ صَالِحٍ يَدْعُو لَهُ

The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "When a person dies, his deeds come to an end except for three: ongoing charity (sadaqah jariyah), knowledge from which people benefit, or a righteous child who prays for him."

Sahih Muslim

مَنْ دَلَّ عَلَى خَيْرٍ فَلَهُ مِثْلُ أَجْرِ فَاعِلِهِ

The Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever guides to something good will have a reward like the one who does it."

Sahih Muslim

مَا نَقَصَتْ صَدَقَةٌ مِنْ مَالٍ

The Prophet ﷺ said: "Charity does not decrease wealth."

Sahih Muslim

Why your gift matters

Sirajul Ummah Islamic Institute teaches Qur'an, Islamic studies and useful skills to students near and far. Your sadaqah helps us keep lessons affordable, add new courses and books, and reach students who could not otherwise learn. Every person who benefits from this knowledge is a continuing reward for you, in sha Allah.

A dua for you

آجَرَكَ اللَّهُ فِيمَا أَعْطَيْتَ، وَبَارَكَ لَكَ فِيمَا أَبْقَيْتَ، وَجَعَلَهُ لَكَ طَهُورًا

رَبَّنَا تَقَبَّلْ مِنَّا إِنَّكَ أَنتَ السَّمِيعُ الْعَلِيمُ

“Our Lord, accept this from us. You are the All-Hearing, the All-Knowing.”

Qur’an, Surah Al-Baqarah 2:127
