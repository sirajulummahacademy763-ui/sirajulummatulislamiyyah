Privacy policy

We respect your privacy. This page explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Last updated: 12 October 2026

The short version

  • We collect only what we need to run your student account.
  • We never sell your information.
  • Your data is stored securely with Google Firebase.
  • Passwords are handled by Firebase. We cannot see them.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information at any time.

1What we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, phone number, country, education level and learning interests.
  • Study details: the classes you choose, your coins, and the courses, books and certificates you unlock.
  • Learning records: your answers to assignments, tests and exams, your marks and teacher comments, and your certificates.
  • Messages: what you send through the contact form or to our help assistant.

2How we use it

  • To create and run your student account.
  • To show you the right courses and books for your classes.
  • To mark your work, record your results and issue certificates.
  • To send you notices about terms, classes and the institute.
  • To reply to your messages and keep the site safe.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

3Who can see your information

Administrators of the institute can see student accounts, classes and results. Teachers can see the work and results of students in their own courses. Other students cannot see your details.

4Services we use

We use trusted services to run the site, and they may process some information for us:

  • Google Firebase for sign-in and for storing account and learning data.
  • Paystack for card or bank payments. Your card details go directly to Paystack and are never seen or stored by us.
  • Jitsi for live video classes.
  • Web3Forms to deliver messages from the contact form to our email.
  • Google Fonts and GitHub Pages to display and host the site.

5Where it is stored and how we protect it

Your details are stored securely with Google Firebase and are protected by access rules, so that each person can see only what they are allowed to see. Passwords are handled by Firebase and are never visible to us. No online service is perfectly secure, so please choose a strong password and keep it private.

6Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's local storage only for simple things such as keeping you signed in and remembering that you have seen a notice. We do not use advertising cookies.

7Children

Children may learn with us with the knowledge and permission of a parent or guardian. Parents may contact us at any time to see, correct or delete their child's information.

8How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active, and for as long as needed to keep your learning records and certificates. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete your personal details, except what we must keep by law or to verify a certificate we issued.

9Your choices and rights

You may ask us to see, correct or delete your information, or to stop using it for a purpose you do not agree with. Email us at sirajulummahacademy763@gmail.com and we will reply as soon as we can. We follow the data protection laws that apply in Nigeria.

10Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Questions?

Write to us at sirajulummahacademy763@gmail.com or use the contact page.
